# Shopping list

Nothing here blocks M0 to M4. Prices are approximate (Thomann.no ships to Norway with NOK pricing at checkout).

| Item | Why | Recommendation | Approx. price | Needed by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| USB-MIDI interface (1 in / 1 out) | Neither the HeadRush nor the Black Spirit exposes USB-MIDI; rig control needs 5-pin DIN from the Mac | **Roland UM-ONE mk2** (plug-and-play on macOS, reliable); budget option Thomann MIDI USB 1x1 (about €15, mixed reviews) | 300 to 400 kr | M5 |
| 2 × 5-pin MIDI cables (1 to 3 m) | Interface → Black Spirit In; Black Spirit Out/Thru → HeadRush In | Any | 100 to 200 kr | M5 |
| 1 × 1/4-inch TRS cable (short) | Latency calibration loopback (output → guitar input), once per device | Any | 50 to 100 kr | M1e |
| Bluetooth page-turner pedal (optional) | Hands-free push-to-talk for Jo and next-section, without changing rigs | PageFlip Firefly, Donner or similar (sends keyboard keys) | 400 to 800 kr | M2 |
| Headset or dynamic mic (optional) | Jo near a loud cabinet; the MacBook mic works at moderate volume | Any dynamic mic on the Scarlett, or a USB headset | 300 to 1000 kr | M2 |
| Alternative to the two items above: Scarlett 4i4 4th gen | Has MIDI DIN in/out, replacing the separate MIDI box, plus loopback and more I/O | Only if he wants to retire the 2i2 | about 2500 kr | M5 |
