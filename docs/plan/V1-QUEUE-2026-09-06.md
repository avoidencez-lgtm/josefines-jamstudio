## Shared assignment contract

- **Authority/stop:** one bounded observable outcome, one thematic PR; author stops only after required exact-head CI, review repairs within scope and handoff. Review runs after author stops. No merge, issue closure, asset/release publication, paid provider call or another model launch without the relevant explicit authority. #274 remains protected by the present no-merge boundary.
- **Gates:** lint/types/frontend tests/build/licenses; fmt/Clippy/workspace tests/deny; S4 probe fmt/Clippy/tests/deny; gitleaks/offline links; native embedded build/smoke Windows+macOS; applicable measured acceptance in the linked R rows. Ponytail review and staged diff check before commit. Do not weaken a failing guard or bypass OS policy.
- **Harness:** verify exact model, installed harness/version, effective instructions, workspace/base and account availability at dispatch. Only Astra high reasoning is fixed; other reasoning settings must be recorded from the supported native harness, not invented. No six simultaneous sessions or custom orchestrator.
- **Fallback:** after two unsuccessful repairs without a supported root cause, preserve evidence and hand off to the listed fallback; do not launch it automatically. Reviewer is independent of author. Clock/recorder/MIDI contract changes always stay with Astra, even if a UI/model task discovers them.
- **Evidence:** fixture provenance and output hashes, named checks with exit/status, exact CI SHA/URLs, native versus preview, synthetic versus recorded/live, missing prerequisites, next authorized action. No real user library in tests.
